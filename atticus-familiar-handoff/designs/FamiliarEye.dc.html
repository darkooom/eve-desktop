01 · Anatomy

One body, one eye

Body
Metaball, Volt #D4FF3A. Shape = mood.
Pupil
Ink #0E0F0C, 27% of the body. Position = attention.
Catchlight
Always top right, never moves. Keeps it alive.
Satellite
Orbits once per finished step.
02 · Gaze = where to look

The eye always looks at what matters right now

At youListening. Looks out of the screen, at the person talking.
Up and awayThinking. Glances up right, like recalling something.
ScanningSearching. Sweeps left ↔ right, 1.4s per pass.
At the workWorking. Fixed on the mini screen and follows each click there.
At the riskNeeds you. Points at the recipient or the file list.
03 · Expressions

Mood lives in the body and the lid

IdleLid half down, breathes every 4s.
Blink120ms, every 3–7s at random, also on each new step.
PleasedDone. Squints, hops 4px, then turns into ✓.
AlertApproval. Stretches up, pupil shrinks, holds still.
SadError. Sags 10%, turns grey, lid droops.
ShrugDenied or expired. Small sideways wobble.
PausedHollow body. Not listening, mic off.
Safe mode offPermanent badge, so it is never forgotten.
